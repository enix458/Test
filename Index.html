1 · Movement
DRAGDrag from your sector to a linked sector. Roads only. Tap any sector to inspect its garrison.
½ / ALLSets how much of the garrison marches. Every arm present is committed in proportion.
2 · Combined Arms
MIXEDA sector holds infantry, armour and artillery together. Units keep their arm wherever they march — a tank battalion stays a tank battalion.
OUTPUTEach sector produces its own arm no matter who holds it. Take an artillery sector and it starts feeding guns into your stack.
INFANTRY  ▸  beats ARMOUR
ARMOUR    ▸  beats ARTILLERY
ARTILLERY ▸  beats INFANTRY
ODDSFavourable ×1.5, unfavourable ×0.75, resolved arm against arm across the whole mix. A balanced stack is never badly countered. A pure stack hits far harder when it guesses right and collapses when it guesses wrong.
3 · Concentric Assault
MASSINGColumns that reach the same sector within about two seconds of each other strike as one force, at the echelon of their combined strength. Three battalions landing together hit as a division.
TIMINGArmour marches fastest, artillery slowest. Launch from your far sector first if you want them to arrive together.
WHYA dug-in defender beats attacks that arrive one at a time. Piecemeal attack is how armies lose.
4 · Echelon
II · BNUnder 12 total. No bonus. Fast.
III · REGT12 to 35. ×1.2 in combat.
XX · DIV36 and up. ×1.45, but slow to march.
5 · Supply
HQEach army has one headquarters, marked with a flag. Supply runs from HQ along roads through friendly sectors only.
CUT OFFAn unsupplied sector is hatched in red. It produces nothing, loses entrenchment, fights at ×0.8 and bleeds strength until relieved or destroyed.
WARNINGEmptying a rear sector can sever the whole front behind it. A raid deep into enemy ground cuts itself off the moment it lands.
BREAKOUTAn encircled garrison can still attack, but only toward ground that would restore its supply.
DISTANCEOutput falls the further a sector sits from its HQ, down to half. Sprawling conquests deliver less than a compact front.
HQ LOSTIf a headquarters falls, command displaces to that army's strongest surviving sector.
6 · Victory
WINHold every sector.
LOSEHold none.
